# Structured student episode overview

Use a consistent lab header and four fixed finding slots across labs: most
errors, fewest errors, direct requests, and testing after the first reply.
Templates and numbers are computed from the loaded records; missing
classifications are shown as unavailable. The lab tutor-use headline counts
distinct students. Question measures still count one student per question.

Purple means higher recorded workload within the lab (median time or median
errors above the median across questions); blue means lower or equal workload.
These are relative observations, not ratings of inherent question complexity.
Red highlights errors and direct requests for instructor review. Green marks
passing checks and later testing; neither color establishes learning or intent.
Every color also has a text label. Sequence evidence remains available in the
side panel with all events in order and optional technical fields.
