## Adapter defect and limits

A synthetic contract probe demonstrated a false acceptance: a known
`last_assistance` observation could cite only a tutor turn and still make a record
accepted. The regression failed before the fix. The adapter now requires student
speaker evidence for that context field; a tutor's invitation cannot stand in for
the student's expressed request. None of the six real records supplies that field,
so the fix does not change their six-partial result. A second regression preserves
coarse human references when fine labels and matching context are absent.

No false accept/reject was found on these six normalized records. That is not an
exhaustive validation. The adapter alone still cannot authenticate a source or
reviewer, verify label semantics, establish natural course use, certify account
inventory completeness, or infer student status. Hash-consistent invented claims
remain possible without upstream verification. The private normalizer supplies
that local traceability layer for this fixed selection, not a general database
adapter. It retains the prior audit's source assertions rather than requerying the
original service. Assistant agreement remains exploratory, not accuracy.

The private field `empirical_reference` means a candidate original-rubric reference
with supplied recorded/human provenance. It must not be read as a validity verdict.
In this validation only the two original human coarse observations qualify;
no fine observation is promoted and no record enters the sampler.
